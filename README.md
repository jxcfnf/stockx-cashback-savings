## Buy Now or place a Bid?

StockX uses a live marketplace model. Buyers can purchase at the current asking price or submit a Bid that sellers may accept.

### Buy Now

Buy Now is the faster option. You pay the current available ask for the selected size or product listing, then continue to checkout.

It makes sense when:

- The price is already close to recent sale prices.
- The item is in high demand.
- You need the product by a particular date.
- The current ask is lower than comparable listings elsewhere.
- You do not want to wait for a seller to accept a Bid.

The downside is that you accept the market price currently available. A discount code may reduce the total, but it does not change whether the underlying ask is good.

### Bid

A Bid lets you set the amount you are willing to pay. If a seller accepts it, the transaction proceeds at that price, subject to applicable fees and taxes.

Bidding makes more sense when:

- You are flexible about delivery timing.
- The item has several active sellers.
- The current ask is noticeably above recent sale prices.
- You are willing to wait for a better entry point.
- The product is not moving rapidly.

A Bid is not a guaranteed discount. It is a price offer. The seller can reject it, ignore it, or accept another buyer’s offer first.

A useful approach is to review the product’s recent sale history, compare the lowest ask with recent accepted prices, and choose a Bid that reflects the market rather than picking a random number. Cashback can still apply to an eligible order, but the qualifying amount is based on the confirmed transaction.
